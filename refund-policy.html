Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Before payment

Before making payment, please confirm the programme, fee, batch timing, access conditions and applicable cancellation or refund terms with RV Consulting.

Cancellation request

To request cancellation, contact RV Consulting on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your name, registered phone number, programme name and payment reference. Requests are reviewed against the terms communicated for that programme at enrolment.

Digital material and course access

Once access to digital study material, recordings or course content has been provided, cancellation or refund eligibility may be limited, except where required by applicable law or where the service differs materially from what was agreed.

Service concern

If you believe a paid service was not delivered as agreed, contact us promptly with the relevant details. RV Consulting will review the concern and communicate the available resolution, which may include correction, rescheduling or refund where applicable.

Contact

For cancellation or refund questions, contact RV Consulting on WhatsApp or call +91 97177 66543.

This policy must be reviewed and finalised for your actual programme terms before payment collection. Consumer rights available under applicable law are not limited by this page.
