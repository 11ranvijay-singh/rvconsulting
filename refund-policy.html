Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Before payment

Please confirm the programme, fee, batch timing, instalment arrangement and access conditions with RV Consulting before making payment. By completing payment, you acknowledge this cancellation and refund policy.

Cancellation

You may contact RV Consulting if you need to cancel your enrolment or discuss your course access. Please share your name, registered phone number, programme name and payment reference so the team can assist you with the next steps.

Refund policy

All fees paid for courses, batches, study material, digital content and related services are non-refundable. This applies if you choose not to continue, are unable to attend, change your mind or do not use the course access.

Instalment payments

If an instalment arrangement is approved for your course, each paid instalment is non-refundable. Any remaining payment schedule must be discussed directly with RV Consulting.

Contact us

For help with your enrolment, access or payment schedule, contact RV Consulting on WhatsApp or call +91 97177 66543. The team will guide you respectfully on the available next steps.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law. Please have this policy reviewed by a qualified legal adviser for your business and jurisdiction.
